Step-by-Step Workflow

How LifeDrop BD Works

A streamlined, transparent connection between voluntary donors, patients, and healthcare providers.

1
Register Profile

Create an account as a Donor, Requester, or Hospital. Specify blood group, Bangladesh district, upazila, and contact details.

2
Create or Search

Requesters submit emergency blood requests with hospital and urgency levels. The system queries PostgreSQL for compatible donors.

3
Intelligent Match

The platform filters by ABO/Rh red-cell compatibility matrix, geographic proximity, and conservative 120-day eligibility rules.

4
Certify & Log

Authorized hospital staff or administrators certify the completed donation. PostgreSQL triggers update donor records and audit logs atomically.

Donation Eligibility Rules (Bangladesh Standard)

  • Age: Between 18 and 60 years old.
  • Interval: Minimum 120 days (4 months) between whole blood donations.
  • Weight: Minimum 45 kg (for females) and 50 kg (for males).
  • Hemoglobin: Minimum 12.5 g/dL.
  • Blood Pressure: Systolic 100-140 mmHg, Diastolic 60-90 mmHg.
  • Health: Free from infectious diseases, acute fevers, or antibiotic treatment.
