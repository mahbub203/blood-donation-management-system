Transfusion Intelligence

Understanding ABO & Rh Compatibility

A successful blood transfusion requires careful matching of antigens and antibodies. While O- is recognized as the universal red-cell donor and AB+ as the universal recipient, our system automatically computes valid donor-recipient pairs across all 8 blood groups.

Medical Advisory: Compatibility suggestions in LifeDrop BD are preliminary algorithm matches. Final compatibility must always be crossmatched in a hospital laboratory before transfusion.
