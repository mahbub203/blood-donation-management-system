About LifeDrop BD

Bridging the Gap in Bangladesh's Blood Supply

LifeDrop BD is an advanced, database-driven healthcare informatics platform dedicated to solving the acute blood shortage and emergency response challenges across Bangladesh.

Our Mission

To eliminate preventable maternal, surgical, and trauma deaths caused by delayed access to safe, compatible blood in Bangladesh.

DBMS Innovation

Powered by PostgreSQL relational database architecture, incorporating 3NF schemas, stored procedures, audit triggers, and real-time subquery matching.

Ethical Safety

Donor privacy protection, role-based security access control (RBAC), and conservative 120-day donation interval validation.

Bangladesh Scenario & Significance

According to the World Health Organization (WHO) and the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) of Bangladesh, over 900,000 to 1,000,000 units of blood are required annually in the country. A significant fraction of required blood still relies on replacement donations from desperate relatives. LifeDrop BD coordinates voluntary non-remunerated donors and connects them directly with patients and authorized hospitals across all 64 districts.

Academic & Clinical Disclaimer

LifeDrop BD is engineered as an academic Database Management System (DBMS) capstone project. System-calculated eligibility is a conservative baseline (default 120-day interval). Actual transfusion safety and clinical donor fitness must always be verified by licensed medical professionals through laboratory screening (HIV, HBV, HCV, Syphilis, Malaria) and physical examination.
